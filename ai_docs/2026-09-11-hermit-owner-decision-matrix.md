## Decision and execution checks

- Record the chosen option for every row by full URL. A closure needs its content reason; a repair or reconstruction needs an owner and a fresh exact-head review/validation path.
- Do not treat a proposed closure as proof that the branch was bad. The stricter-route, descendant-pin, changed-policy, obsolete-stack, and consolidation reasons above are distinct.
- Do not treat a prepared branch, a clean mergeability state, an old validation, or this matrix as landing clearance.
- Preserve the unchanged thresholds and populations named above. In particular, do not reduce the 32-test gate, the 32-seed/16-distinct bar, comparator scope, failure classification, or current qualification rules to make a candidate pass.
- Refresh the fully paginated open-PR census after decisions are executed. Zero means the remote pull requests are merged or closed with their recorded reasons, not merely represented by local or append-only branches.
