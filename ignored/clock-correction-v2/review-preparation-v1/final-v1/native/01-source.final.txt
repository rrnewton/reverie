## Determinism and Linux/POSIX

The change introduces no scheduler, run-queue, turn, INFO, or replay modification. Eligibility changes only for the command’s first committed exec. Before that transition, no physical timer arrival can influence delivery; afterward, existing exact target/overshoot handling applies. The grounded Hermit scheduler therefore receives the same classes of Tool requests, with corrected ptrace clock coordinates rather than a new ordering policy.

Linux semantics are preserved: `enable_on_exec` supplies the real successful-exec boundary; failed exec remains returning; exact pending syscalls retain kernel execution; replacements suppress the original once; no-pending injection does not replay guest code; and legacy-vsyscall handling remains explicit.
