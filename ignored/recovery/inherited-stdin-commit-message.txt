Stabilize inherited KVM stdin identity

[hermit2, degraded-unresolved, gpt-5.6-sol, devbig014, role=impl]

Register caller-supplied pipe or socket stdin in the deterministic file-object
identity table before publishing guest state. Preserve that identity across
fork and exec, while dropping it for CLOEXEC and final process exit.

Release exited executors' local identity references after detaching their file
table, so live fork or CLONE_FILES owners retain identity but a kernel-only SCM
queue does not become an implicit identity escrow. Leave ordinary stdin and
the captured-output identity domain unchanged.
