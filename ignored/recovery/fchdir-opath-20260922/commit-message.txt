Support O_PATH directories in KVM fchdir

[hermit2, degraded-unresolved, gpt-5.6-sol, devbig014, role=impl]

Resolve fchdir sources through capture-aware descriptor translation, consume the
Linux low descriptor word, and accept O_PATH directories while retaining an
independent cwd handle. Validate non-directory type before the fallible reopen
so regular files and captured FIFOs keep ENOTDIR under host fd pressure.

Remove the former KVM-only test exemption and cover direct, duplicate,
proc-reopened, high-word, post-close relative-I/O, procfs, capture isolation,
and resource-order cases with required-KVM and anti-skip checks.
