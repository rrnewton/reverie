Add deterministic KVM proc persona providers

[hermit2, degraded-unresolved, gpt-5.6-sol, devbig014, role=impl]

Serve the overflow UID/GID sysctls from the fixed guest identity, generate a
repeatable sequence of distinct UUIDv4 snapshots, and report guest-owned PTY
master descriptions without leaking the host-global PTY count. Preserve the
authenticated synthetic-proc carrier path across direct opens, proc-fd
reopens, aliases, fork, exec, and SCM_RIGHTS transfer.

The immutable carrier model snapshots UUID and PTY content at each successful
readable open. This intentionally does not claim native or Detcore first-read
timing, nor native UUID refresh-after-rewind behavior.
