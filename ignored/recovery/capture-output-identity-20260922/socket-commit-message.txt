Isolate captured KVM socket operations

[hermit2, degraded-unresolved, gpt-5.6-sol, devbig014, role=impl]

Resolve captured stdout and stderr through private carriers for every
supported socket operation, including accept and serialized receive paths.
Reject labelled and metadata-lost capture carriers in SCM_RIGHTS before a
host message can be sent.

Match Linux low-word decoding and socket error/copy ordering, including
post-consumption address-length validation and recvmmsg input validation.
Add native and repeated KVM controls that keep unrelated supervisor socket
options, queues, addresses, and pending connections unchanged.
