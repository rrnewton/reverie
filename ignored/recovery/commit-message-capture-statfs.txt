Stabilize captured KVM pipefs metadata

[hermit2, degraded-unresolved, gpt-5.6-sol, devbig014, role=impl]

Route statfs and fstatfs for captured stdout and stderr through their private
pipe keepers instead of the supervisor's inherited descriptors. This preserves
the synthetic pipe model when the invoking harness uses regular files or has
closed its standard descriptors, while retaining existing deterministic fsid
and capacity normalization.

Add native-oracle, regular-carrier, ordinary-file, alias, proc-fd, fork,
copyout-error, direct, Tool, repeat, and required-KVM anti-skip coverage.
