The new drain takes the executor's whole retained queue once before any consumer is polled. The resulting iterator, returned-error vector, and panic vector stay outside each individual catch_owned_future. Every consumer is awaited exactly once through that helper; caught polling or destruction panics do not short-circuit the loop. A returned typed error is retained unless it is exactly the bare Error::RunAborted variant. SharedFailure, WithCleanup, Cleanup, and other wrappers are not filtered by their primary cause. Original panic payload Boxes are moved into the result in consumer order and, within a consumer, polling-then-destruction order. The final output is always Some(Error::combine(errors)), including an empty queue or a queue that only panicked.

No publication or resume occurs in the production drain. A Some(Ok(())) output accompanied by panic payloads is not an overall successful guest or owner result; the caller must process those payloads and preserve its original panic and diagnostics. Root owns module wiring, actual worker recovery, failure integration, and that caller interpretation. The ordinary consumer path remains unchanged; this task does not complete root, fork, or ordinary cleanup panic routing.
