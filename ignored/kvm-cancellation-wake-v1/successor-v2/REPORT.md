`GuestThreadGroup` owns one shared pending oneshot generation. `subscribe_cancellation` clones that generation; the caller must subscribe before rechecking its existing exit/cancellation predicates and take a fresh subscription after every completed hint. Both cancellation producers first retain their exact existing status/flag/start-gate/SIGURG behavior, explicitly release the worker registry guard, replace the pending generation under the separate wake mutex, release that mutex, and send the old notification. Wake callbacks can therefore reenter subscription and observe a new pending receiver. Readiness (including sender destruction) is only a recheck hint, never cancellation authority or entry permission.

A late subscriber after publication sees the authoritative predicate rather than inheriting an already-ready notification. A root that ignores worker-only cancellation can subscribe again without spinning on that old notification. Exec rearm needs no generation reset: the previous cancellation already installed its pending successor, and subscriptions taken before or after rearm observe the next cancellation. Existing cancel/join-before-rearm ownership remains required; this primitive does not make concurrent rearm safe.
