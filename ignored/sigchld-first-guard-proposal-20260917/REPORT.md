## Proposed behavior and ordering

- `Config::backend_rejects_host_signals` is serde-defaulted false and skipped by clap. `prepare_backend_config` always overwrites it from the chosen backend: true only for KVM, independent of DBT's pidfd requirement. Existing backend behavior and public command syntax are retained.
- `KvmSignalRefusal` preserves the raw Linux signal, selected virtual `DetTid`, and scheduler turn as a typed error. `Scheduler::reject_host_signal` checks the explicit flag first. On the first refusal it closes an existing tentative selection under the same mutex and stores the typed cause. An existing backend failure or first refusal is not overwritten.
- `fire_alarm` refuses after existing target selection and before its physical-delivery INFO message. `signal_guest` also guards independently before its physical-delivery DEBUG message, pidfd lookup, pidfd_send_signal, raw-PID kill or scheduler wake. Thus an accidentally present pidfd cannot bypass KVM's prohibition. Native target selection and diagnostics stay unchanged.
- `step2_process_blocked` now stops immediately after a timed refusal, before ready external IO, deferred SIGCHLD admissions or step 3. The empty-queue deadline path retains its actual `add_extra_time` and existing SkipTurn. `do_a_turn_blocking` captures the result and existing failure sender, unlocks, publishes once and only then returns the error. It cannot lose publication through an early `?` on the deadline path.
- Stacktrace delivery uses the same guard for named and raw realtime signals. Its mutex scope ends before notification and before any further await. On refusal the ordinary RPC enters the existing terminal `lock_rpc_scheduler(false)` wait; it produces neither Continue nor ThreadExited. The established backend failure listener and pending-RPC RunAborted route remain unchanged. Waking by itself would not preserve a cause or stop grants; both are supplied here.
- `GlobalState::join_internal_scheduler` naturally consumes its owned handle without printing a summary. `signal_refusal()` retains the typed cause through poisoned-mutex recovery. Failed cleanup preserves the refusal, JoinError and requested recording result separately.
- `finish_kvm_tool_completion` joins before inspecting the final cause and before calling the success-publishing `clean_up`. Backend Ok plus retained refusal becomes a failure without manufacturing a backend error. The actual join result survives the subsequent cleanup call. Backend errors, including aggregated cleanup errors, remain typed in the chain.
- Refusal alone or refusal accompanied only by the derived bare RunAborted is PolicyRefusal. An independent backend error, scheduler JoinError or recording failure remains Error while preserving the refusal. Transparent Reverie Tool wrappers cannot hide that distinction. This precedence prevents an unrelated internal failure being renamed a policy-only refusal.

The CLI portion is also retained independently under `cli-part/`. Its source-only report is SHA256 `8646b104262b4224116c66008c75cda4d7b350e61a9f6a3bbda345988fae2277`; its complete three-file patch is SHA256 `1f31b460d84dedefe9b0efabe81bb09501712fdc61da7999e90b9fd1a9db2c91`.
