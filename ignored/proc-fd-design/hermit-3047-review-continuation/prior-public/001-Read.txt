6	# Code review
7	
8	## Purpose
9	
10	Try to refute the change's correctness claims. Inspect the implementation, its
11	tests, and the evidence together. A passing suite is not sufficient when the
12	change also weakens what the suite requires.
13	
14	This skill owns the substance of adversarial code review. Separate skills may
15	set reviewer counts, exact-head attestation, validation authority, and landing
16	policy.
17	
18	## Establish the review target
19	
20	Before reviewing, identify the repository, base revision, exact head revision,
21	and intended behavior. Review the complete diff and the relevant surrounding
22	code and tests. Bind the verdict to the exact head; a changed head requires a
23	fresh review.
24	
25	## Mandatory goalpost-moving check
26	
27	The coordinator applies this check before dispatching an adversarial review and
28	includes this complete block in every adversarial-review prompt, not a pointer
29	to it:
30	
31	> GOALPOST-MOVING REVIEW RULE
32	>
33	> Adversarial reviewers must look explicitly for goalpost moving. We are extremely skeptical of any goalpost moving. YOU DO NOT CLEAR THE BAR BY SIMPLY LOWERING THE BAR.
34	>
35	> Treat each of these as an explicit review target:
36	> - weakening an assertion so a test passes
37	> - widening a tolerance · adding an exemption · skipping a case · relaxing a comparator
38	> - renaming or relabelling so a failure reads as a pass
39	> - deleting a check rather than satisfying it
40	
41	Compare every changed test, threshold, allowlist, skip, label, classification,
42	and gate with the base revision. Determine whether the implementation now meets
43	the original requirement or whether the requirement was changed to admit the
44	implementation. Call out either result explicitly, including when no lowering
45	of the bar is present.
46	
47	## Review method
48	
49	1. Trace the changed behavior through the real execution path. Do not infer
50	   behavior from names, configuration, or successful exit status alone.
51	2. Check the claimed evidence against the exact behavior it is supposed to
52	   prove. Identify missing identity, causation, coverage, or provenance links.
53	3. Inspect production and test changes together. Require a qualifying case to
54	   pass and a violating case to be refused when the change introduces or alters
55	   a guard.
56	4. Report concrete findings with file and line evidence, impact, and the change
57	   required to resolve them. Do not substitute a vague procedural objection for
58	   a technical finding.
59	5. State a verdict only after resolving the goalpost-moving check and the
60	   correctness findings against the exact reviewed head.
61	
62	## Report structure
63	
64	```text
65	Review target: <repository> <base>..<exact head>
66	
67	Findings:
68	- <severity>: <file:line> — <problem, impact, and required change>
69	
70	Goalpost-moving assessment:
71	- Assertions weakened: yes/no — <evidence>
72	- Tolerance widened, exemption added, case skipped, or comparator relaxed: yes/no — <evidence>
73	- Failure renamed or relabelled as a pass: yes/no — <evidence>
74	- Check deleted instead of satisfied: yes/no — <evidence>
75	
76	Verification: <commands or evidence inspected, with limitations>
77	Verdict: approve / changes requested
78	```
79	
80	Put findings first. If there are no findings, say so explicitly and still
81	include the goalpost-moving assessment, residual risks, and verification limits.
